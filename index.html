A solo-mined meme experiment. One wallet, no team, no pool, no social media.

Abstract

A lone miner can still hit a block against all odds. This experiment asks the same question of a token: with no team, no roadmap and no promotion, can a stranger find it and hold it?

There is nothing to join and nothing to follow. The coin lives on a single platform, and this page is its only documentation.
